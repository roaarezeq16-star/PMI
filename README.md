# PMI — Project Management Institute: Highest Global Standards

## Overview

The **Project Management Institute (PMI)** is the world's leading professional organization for project management, setting the highest global standards for project delivery, business governance, and organizational excellence. PMI's frameworks and certifications are recognized in over 200 countries and empower professionals to drive value through disciplined, ethical, and strategic project management.

---

## Core Global Standards

### 1. PMBOK® Guide (Project Management Body of Knowledge)
The foundational standard for project management, defining best practices across the full project lifecycle:
- **Initiating** — Define project scope, stakeholders, and objectives.
- **Planning** — Develop schedules, budgets, risk plans, and resource strategies.
- **Executing** — Lead teams, manage quality, and coordinate work packages.
- **Monitoring & Controlling** — Track progress, control changes, and ensure deliverable quality.
- **Closing** — Formally complete the project and capture lessons learned.

### 2. PMI Standard for Risk Management in Portfolios, Programs, and Projects
Establishes a structured, repeatable approach to identifying, analyzing, and responding to risk across all levels of the organization.

### 3. PMI Standard for Scheduling
Provides globally recognized principles for creating and managing project schedules to ensure on-time delivery.

### 4. PMI Standard for Earned Value Management (EVM)
Integrates scope, schedule, and cost performance to give an accurate, objective view of project health.

### 5. PMI Standard for Organizational Project Management (OPM)
Aligns project, program, and portfolio management with organizational strategy to maximize business value.

### 6. The Standard for Program Management
Guides the coordinated management of related projects to achieve benefits not available from managing them individually.

### 7. The Standard for Portfolio Management
Ensures that the right programs and projects are selected and prioritized to deliver the organization's strategic objectives.

### 8. Agile Practice Guide (co-developed with Agile Alliance)
Provides practical guidance for applying agile approaches to deliver value iteratively, including Scrum, Kanban, and hybrid models.

## Key Principles (PMI's 12 Project Management Principles — PMBOK 7th Edition)

1. **Stewardship** — Be a diligent, respectful, and caring steward.
2. **Team** — Create a collaborative project team environment.
3. **Stakeholders** — Effectively engage with stakeholders.
4. **Value** — Focus on value delivery throughout the project.
5. **Systems Thinking** — Recognize, evaluate, and respond to system interactions.
6. **Leadership** — Demonstrate leadership behaviors.
7. **Tailoring** — Tailor your approach based on context.
8. **Quality** — Build quality into processes and deliverables.
9. **Complexity** — Navigate complexity with skill and adaptability.
10. **Risk** — Optimize risk responses.
11. **Adaptability & Resilience** — Embrace adaptability and resilience.
12. **Change** — Enable change to achieve the envisioned future state.

## Business Standards & Governance Best Practices

- **Establish clear project governance** with defined roles, decision rights, and escalation paths.
- **Align projects to strategic objectives** using a portfolio management approach.
- **Implement robust change management** to handle scope, schedule, and cost changes effectively.
- **Drive continuous improvement** through retrospectives, lessons learned, and process audits.
- **Foster a culture of ethical conduct** in line with PMI's Code of Ethics & Professional Conduct.
- **Measure and report value** using KPIs, OKRs, and earned value metrics.
- **Adopt hybrid delivery models** that blend predictive and agile methods as appropriate.
- **Ensure stakeholder engagement** at all levels throughout the project lifecycle.
